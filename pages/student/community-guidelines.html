Community Guidelines

How we keep Campus Space respectful and useful for everyone.

Be respectful

Treat every member with courtesy. Disagreement is fine. Personal attacks are not. Harassment, bullying, hate speech and threats are not tolerated and will lead to suspension.

Stay on topic

Post content that is useful or relevant to the campus community. Spam, unrelated advertising and repeated off-topic posts are removed.

Be honest

Do not mislead others. False information — especially about exams, deadlines, fees or safety — harms everyone.

Respect privacy

Do not share personal information about others without their consent. Do not screenshot private conversations or complaints.

Report problems

If you see content that breaks these guidelines, use the report option on that post. Reports are reviewed by Admin. Your identity is kept private.

Consequences

Depending on the severity of a breach, Admin may hide content, delete content, warn the author, or suspend the account. Repeated breaches may result in permanent restriction.
