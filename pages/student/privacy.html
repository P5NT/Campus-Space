Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Our commitment

Campus Space treats your personal data with care. This page explains what we collect, why, and how you control it.

What we collect

  • Your name, username, phone number and email
  • Your faculty, department, level and matric number
  • Content you post in the community
  • Complaints you submit (visible only to you and Admin)
  • Notification and preference settings

Who can see your data

By default:

  • Public to other students: name, username, faculty, department, level (if you enable)
  • Private by default: matric number, phone number
  • Visible only to you and Admin: complaints, reports, verification records

You can change what is public from Settings → Privacy.

Your rights

  • Access your own data at any time
  • Correct information that is out of date
  • Control public visibility
  • Deactivate your account
  • Request deletion of your account and data

Security

Passwords are protected. Sessions expire automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity. Sensitive actions require confirmation.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Campus Space team via the Contact page.
